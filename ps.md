# Inventory and Stock Maintenance Management System
Design and develop an automated inventory and stock management system that accurately tracks stock levels in real time, reduces manual errors, and improves efficiency in inventory control and decision-making.

## Objectives 
To automate inventory management: Reduce manual record-keeping by maintaining inventory data electronically for better accuracy and efficiency.
To track stock levels in real time: Monitor available stock, incoming items, and outgoing items to avoid overstocking or stock shortages.
To manage product information efficiently: Store and update details such as product name, category, quantity, price, supplier, and expiry date (if applicable).
To improve stock control and planning: Help management make informed purchasing decisions using stock usage trends and reports
To generate reports and analytics: Provide reports on stock availability, sales, purchases, low-stock alerts, and inventory valuation.
To support multiple users with roles: Allow different access levels for admin, manager, and staff to ensure security and accountability.
To ensure timely replenishment of stock: Automatically alert users when stock reaches a minimum reorder level.
To integrate sales and purchase management: Update inventory automatically when sales or purchases occur.
To enhance operational efficiency: Save time, reduce costs, and improve overall business productivity through streamlined inventory processes.
